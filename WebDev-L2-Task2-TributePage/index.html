Cristiano Ronaldo

Twenty-three years in the number 7 shirt of Portugal.

The player who never stopped

Cristiano Ronaldo was born in 1985 in Funchal, on the island of Madeira, into a family with very little money. What he did have was a ball, a stubborn streak and a talent that was impossible to ignore. By his late teens he had moved from Sporting CP in Lisbon to Manchester United, and Portugal had found the player it would build its hopes around for two decades.

He made his debut for the national team in August 2003, aged eighteen. Over the next twenty-three years he grew from a quick, daring winger into the captain, the leading scorer and the face of the team. He played at six World Cups, and he scored in five of them, something no one had done before him.

His finest night in red came at Euro 2016. He limped off injured early in the final in Paris, yet he stayed at the touchline, shouting instructions and encouragement to his teammates. When Portugal won the trophy, it felt like the reward for all the near misses that came before it, including the painful home final at Euro 2004.

The numbers are staggering: 146 goals in 234 matches, both records for Portugal. But the habit behind them matters more. He trained harder than almost anyone, kept his body in shape long after others slow down, and showed a generation of young Portuguese players how far discipline can carry them. In November 2026 he announced his retirement from the national team. He keeps playing at club level, but this page celebrates the years he gave to his country.

Key moments with Portugal

  1. 2003

    Makes his Portugal debut in August at just eighteen years old.

  2. 2004

    Reaches the final of Euro 2004 on home soil, losing to Greece.

  3. 2016

    Captains Portugal to its first major trophy at Euro 2016.

  4. 2019

    Wins the first UEFA Nations League with Portugal.

  5. 2022

    Scores at a fifth World Cup, a first for any men's player.

  6. 2026

    Announces his international retirement on 5 November as Portugal's record scorer and most-capped player.
